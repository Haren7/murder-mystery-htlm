Erica's Call

I got a call from the reception. It was a muffled voice, a man's voice — asking me to go to Nathan's room to find out the truth before I 'made a mistake.'

Scared as hell, I told Anne that I wanted to take a breather before going to sleep.

I ran to Nathan's room and just as I entered the corridor — I saw Margot exiting the same.

Since I had the second key to his room, I went in — only to find Nathan snoring away. He doesn't know this but when he is in deep sleep he tends to say my name. And that's the exact way I found him.

I tried to wake him up but he was in too deep a sleep. I left the room, confused as to why Margot was in the corridor. Did she try to come into his room? Did she want something? Was she meeting someone else on that floor? I guess we won't know. But one thing is for sure — the person who called me did not know my trust for Nathan. I knew of his old crush on Margot, but I also know his immense love for me.
